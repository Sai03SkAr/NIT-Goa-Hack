03

Top level: synthetic identity fraud. It gets through in three ways. A forged identity: an AI-made or edited card, or a real ID with someone else’s face; we trust the issuer’s signed QR and the issuer’s photo, not the pixels. Faked presence: deepfakes, replayed selfies, bots; we use a server-issued head-turn challenge, C2PA AI-provenance and typing rhythm. Fraud at scale: rings and repeat use across banks; we link applications in an identity graph and share keyed hashes between banks.

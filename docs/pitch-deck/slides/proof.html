On a time-based test split of 444 applications with 85 fraud cases, every fraud was caught. The fused system reaches ROC-AUC 0.998 against 0.78 for the document check alone. 95% of rejections were real fraud. An adaptive attacker that optimises what it controls got 0 of 85 frauds through. The headline comparison: a state-of-the-art pixel forgery detector is at chance on an edited date of birth, while the signed-QR check catches about 95%. The data is synthetic, so we claim relative gains, not real-world accuracy.
